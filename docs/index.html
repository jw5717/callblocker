Call Blocker

It just blocks calls. Nothing else.

Free of modern-day annoyances. No subscriptions, no ads, no tracking — it simply blocks calls.

What it does

  • ✓ Blocks every call that isn't from one of your contacts or a number you've whitelisted inside the app.
  • ✓ Keeps a blocked-call log — see every call it stopped, grouped by day, with the time it came in.
  • ✓ Whitelist any number — let a specific number through without adding it to your contacts.
  • ✓ One-tap on/off — turn blocking on or off whenever you like.

What it does not do

  • ✗ No subscriptions. It's not a rental. There's nothing to pay, ever.
  • ✗ No relentless, annoying ads. None. Not a single one.
  • ✗ No tracking. It doesn't watch anything you do on your phone.
  • ✗ No data harvesting. It doesn't collect your personal or public data — and it certainly doesn't sell it to the highest bidder.
  • ✗ No piggybacking. Other apps can't latch onto it to steal data. In fact, I personally removed this app's ability to make a network connection at all — it physically cannot send anything off your phone.

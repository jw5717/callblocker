# Closed test — tester outreach

Goal: 12+ testers (aim for 14–15) who opt in and stay opted in for 14 consecutive
days. Google counts unique **Google accounts**, not devices.

## Recruitment message (text / email / DM)

> Hey [name] — I built a little Android app called **Simple Call Blocker**. It
> silently blocks calls from anyone who isn't in your contacts — no spam, no
> robocalls, no ads, nothing tracked.
>
> To put it on the Play Store, Google requires 12 people to test it for 2 weeks.
> Would you help? It's basically "install it and forget it":
>
> 1. You need an Android phone + a Google account.
> 2. On your phone, open this link and tap to join: **[OPT-IN LINK]**
> 3. Install "Simple Call Blocker" from the page it opens.
> 4. Leave it installed for 2 weeks (please don't opt out — it resets the clock).
> 5. If anything looks off, just text me.
>
> That's it. Thanks a ton — it genuinely only works if a few friends pitch in!

## Where to get the opt-in link

Play Console → Test and release → Testing → **Closed testing** → your track →
**Testers** tab → add testers (email list or Google Group) → copy the
**"Copy link"** opt-in URL. Paste it into the message above.

## What to ask testers to do (for genuine engagement)

- Actually use their phone normally with it installed.
- Note anything confusing or broken and tell you.
- Stay opted in the full 14 days.

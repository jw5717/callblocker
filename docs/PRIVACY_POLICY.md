# Privacy Policy — Call Blocker

_Last updated: 23 October 2026_

Call Blocker ("the app") is designed around a single principle: your data never
leaves your phone. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do.

## Summary

- The app does **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.
- The app has **no internet access** and is technically incapable of sending
  data anywhere. It does not request the `INTERNET` permission.
- There are **no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs** of any
  kind in the app.
- Everything the app stores stays in the app's private storage on your device.

## Information the app accesses

**Contacts.** The app requests permission to read your contacts
(`READ_CONTACTS`). It uses this for one purpose only: when a call comes in, it
checks whether the caller's number is saved in your contacts so it can decide
whether to let the call through. Your contacts are read on the device, in the
moment, and are never copied, stored, logged, or transmitted.

**Incoming call numbers.** When a call is blocked, the app records the caller's
phone number and the time it was blocked, so you can review it later on the log
screen. This record is stored only in the app's private storage on your device.
It is never transmitted anywhere, and you can clear it at any time from the log
screen.

**Call-screening role.** You may grant the app Android's call-screening role.
This lets the app decide, on the device, whether to allow or reject each
incoming call. No information about your calls is sent off the device.

## Information the app collects

None. The app does not collect any personal or sensitive user data as defined by
Google Play, because no data is ever transmitted off your device.

## Data sharing

None. Because no data is collected or transmitted, there is nothing to share
with any third party.

## Data retention and deletion

The blocked-call log and your whitelist are stored only on your device. You can
delete the blocked-call log at any time using the "Clear" button on the log
screen. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of its data from your
device. Automatic cloud backup is disabled, so this data is never included in
Google backups.

## Children's privacy

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children.
